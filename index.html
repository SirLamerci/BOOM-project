About us

PC BOOM Maintenance is a company specializing in computer maintenance established in São Paulo, Brazil. Offering hardware and software repair services, as well as component upgrades to improve your PC's performance. The idea originated from a simple job assembling computers for friends and family, and since then we have grown to serve a wider clientele. Our commitment is to provide quality service with transparency and respect for the customer.
